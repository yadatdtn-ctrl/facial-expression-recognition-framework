Key finding

Why the custom CNN wins

FER-2013 images are 48×48 grayscale — a very different domain from the high-resolution RGB photos that ImageNet is built from. MobileNetV2 and ResNet50 carry assumptions that do not hold here. This mismatch, known as domain gap, systematically limits their performance. The custom CNN, designed for this input from scratch, avoids these assumptions entirely.
